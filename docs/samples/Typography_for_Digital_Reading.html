The Craft of Digital Typography

Typography exists to honor content. On a handheld screen, comfortable reading depends on three harmonious variables: measure (line width), leading (line height), and contrast.

1. Measure and Line Rhythm

A well-set column contains between 45 and 75 characters per line. When lines are too short, the eye darts back and forth prematurely; when lines are too wide, finding the start of the next line becomes fatiguing.

Typography is what language looks like when it is given room to breathe.

2. Choosing Palettes for Ambient Light

  • Light & Sepia: Ideal for daylight and warm indoor reading, reducing harsh blue contrast.
  • Dark Slate: Balanced low-luminance contrast for evening study.
  • AMOLED Black: Zero-emission background pixels on OLED displays for maximum battery conservation in dark environments.

3. Mathematical & Technical Typesetting

Technical prose mixes continuous narrative with symbolic expressions, code listings, and structured tables. Maintaining consistent vertical spacing before and after display blocks prevents visual crowding.
